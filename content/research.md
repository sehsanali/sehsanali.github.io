## Working papers

###### Probability of formulating the agenda
> We consider a group of agents who submit sets of issues to form an agenda for an eventual collective choice. We provide a sufficient condition for the set aggregation rules and two regularity assumptions on the distribution of sets reported by each agent, such that an agenda is formed with probability one as the group grows arbitrarily large.
